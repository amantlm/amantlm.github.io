🔦 Simple Electric Torch — Circuit Lab Teaching-Learning Material • Closed Circuits & Conduction

🔧 Workbench — click two terminals to connect a wire

📋 How to Use

  1. Click any metal terminal — it will glow orange. Then click a terminal of a DIFFERENT component to draw a wire.
  2. Connect the Cell and Bulb correctly (+ to L1 and - to L2) to make it glow!
  3. Click the red switch lever to turn it ON.
  4. If the connections are correct, the bulb glows! 🔥 Click a wire to remove it and try again.
💡 Did you know? For the bulb to glow properly in this experiment, terminals must match correctly (+ to + and - to -).
